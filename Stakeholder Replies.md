# Stakeholder Replies

---

## 1. CEO

**Subject:** Product Priority Recommendation

Hi [CEO],

I agree AI will matter for Fernwick's future. I believe patrons will come to expect it, and I want us ready quickly, so I took your suggestion seriously.

However, a chatbot shouldn't be our first investment. The $4.2B estimate comes from sponsored research with no surveyed paying deployments. In our own survey, only 5.3% of library staff ranked AI assistance as their top problem, and 57% of them would pay nothing.

With our 18-month payback requirement, I recommend Mobile Printing first, where we already see customer demand, lost deals, and churn. We will use AI tools to build it faster.

I'd make the chatbot our next priority. Once Mobile Printing ships, we can test it with a few libraries and confirm willingness to pay before a full build.

Best,
Vanny

## 2. VP Sales

**Subject:** Events Module and Product Priorities

Hi [VP Sales],

Thank you for raising the Events Module. I understand how frustrating it is to lose deals because we are missing a feature.

I reviewed the request and sales data. Of 329 Events requests, 287 were logged internally, while 40 unique customers requested the feature. We also recorded 21 lost deals worth $45,000 over three years.

By comparison, Mobile Printing was linked to 23 lost deals worth $205,040 in regions we currently serve, including 13 new customer opportunities.

That is why I recommend Mobile Printing first. I am not removing Events from our future plans.


Best,
Vanny

## 3. Customer Care Manager

**Subject:** Pay Support Issues and Next Steps

Hi [Customer Care Manager],

I understand the pressure your team has been facing with Pay. Support tickets increased from about 74 to 540 per month after version 4.2 was released, and I don't want to overlook that.

However, the ticket data suggests that replacing the entire product may not solve the main issues. In the first three months after release, 83.5% of tickets were related to usage questions, unclear instructions, or documentation gaps, while 8.4% were classified as defects.

I recommend addressing the most common workflow and documentation problems first, rather than rebuilding Pay.

Could you share the top recurring issues and what may have caused the increase to 191 tickets in September? I would like to identify improvements we can prioritize within available maintenance resources.

Besy,
Vanny
