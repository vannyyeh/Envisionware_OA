# Decision Journal


## 1. Initial Hypothesis

### My first choice: Mobile Printing

Mobile Printing is my first choice before reviewing the data. The market report shows that 63% of suburban and urban public libraries receive weekly requests for mobile printing, while only 38% currently have a solution. However, libraries without a solution may not have the budget or willingness to purchase one.

I do not know whether Fernwick already offers Mobile Printing. If it does not, this could be a good opportunity to acquire customers before more competitors enter the market.

### Why I did not choose the other options first

#### AI Chatbot

I think AI chatbots have strong long-term potential. Future users, especially younger generations, may increasingly expect AI assistance, and introducing it early could improve long-term retention.

However, the Chatterlane report estimates a $4.2 billion market without surveying any paying deployments, so I question the actual market size and willingness to pay. Since Fernwick must recover its investment within 18 months, I would not prioritize a full AI chatbot product right now, but I would keep it as one of the next priority projects.

#### Reserve SaaS

If Fernwick already provides Mobile Printing, I would prioritize investigating Reserve SaaS. The CFO describes it as the fastest-growing product line, but also mentions renewal losses to BookIt Cloud. I want to understand why they are leaving and what BookIt Cloud has that we do not.

#### Pay

I do not think Fernwick should rebuild its entire Pay product. I would first investigate the support tickets to understand whether the problems with Pay 4.2 are caused by technical defects, unclear workflows, or missing instructions. Targeted improvements may be enough.

#### Events Module

I think the Events Module is a reasonable request, but I would need stronger business evidence. The VP of Sales mentions more than 400 requests, but I want to know how many different customers submitted them and how much potential revenue was lost. AI tools may help us build faster, so I would consider Events for a future release.

---

## 2. After Reviewing the Data

My final choice is still **Mobile Printing**, but my reasoning has changed.

At first, I mainly focused on the market gap and the opportunity to attract new customers. The data showed me that Mobile Printing is also about offering additional services to existing Print customers and reducing customer losses.

I also realized that Reserve has a more serious retention problem than I initially expected, so I considered **Reserve Check-in and No-show Release** as a strong alternative.

I am no longer choosing Mobile Printing simply because the market has growth potential. I am considering actual lost deals, customer demand, potential additional revenue, development costs, and the 18-month payback requirement.

---

## 3. One Place I Disagreed with AI

**AI's suggestion:** Prioritize Reserve because it has a more direct customer retention opportunity, higher historical lost ARR, and potentially lower delivery risk since it builds on an existing product.

**Why I disagreed:**

- Reserve has a real retention problem. However, not all 43 customers who moved to BookIt Cloud necessarily left because of the missing Check-in and No-show Release features, so the historical lost ARR does not mean we can recover it by adding them.
- I place more importance on generating additional revenue, not only protecting existing revenue. Mobile Printing may provide a stronger opportunity for growth, although its return still depends on pricing, integration costs, and the 18-month payback requirement.
