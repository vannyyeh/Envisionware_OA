# AI Usage Log

I used AI tools to support data analysis, product planning, writing review, and prototyping during this assessment.

| Area | How I Used AI | My Review and Decisions |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Analysis** | Used LLMs (ChatGPT and Claude) to explore CSV data, compare product opportunities, and develop TAM, SAM, SOM, and payback calculations. | Reviewed and challenged assumptions, checked revenue sources, and removed overlapping opportunities. |
| **Product Decision** | Used AI to compare Mobile Printing and Reserve, including market potential, customer retention, and delivery risks. | I challenged AI's preference for Reserve and chose Mobile Printing based on stronger evidence of new customer opportunities and upsell potential. |
| **Product Planning** | Used AI to research competitors and review their product workflows. | Independently defined the first release scope and developed acceptance criteria. I also simplified the staff workflow and clarified print job states. |
| **Writing** | Used AI to draft and revise the Decision Memo, First Release, Stakeholder Replies, First Release documentation, and design prompts. | Wrote the Decision Journal independently. |
| **Prototype** | Used Figma Make and Stitch to explore design systems and wireframes, and Codex to develop and refine an interactive desktop web app. | Reviewed usability, color usage, visual hierarchy, user flows, testing, interactions, and error handling. |
